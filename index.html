Select a region, get a greyscale heightmap. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Region

Click Draw rectangle, then drag on the map.

Drag a corner to resize, or the arrows handle in the middle to move the whole rectangle. With a mouse, hold Shift, Ctrl/⌘ or Alt/Option while dragging to resize from the centre.

Preview is 8-bit for display only; the export keeps the full bit depth you chose. Black = lowest (white = lowest if inverted); magenta = no data (exported as black).

The route layer is a transparent RGBA PNG at the same pixel size, with a red line of the width above (flat profile), for overlaying in an image editor.
