5 Export

Export is available once elevation data has loaded.

Route export

These need only the region and the route, not the elevation data.

Transparent PNG with a red line of the width above, framed exactly like the heightmap. A mostly empty image compresses to almost nothing, and it is drawn a strip at a time, so even very large sizes are fine to export (but your CAD/CAM or image editor must hold the whole bitmap to open it).

Select the route together with the border or corner marks and scale the group: the border or marks span exactly the heightmap's extent, so scale that group to the heightmap's width and height (keep the aspect ratio locked), then delete them.

How to edit

  • Move a point: drag it.
  • Add a point: drag the line itself.
  • Select a point: click it. Shift+click another to select the stretch between.
  • Delete the selection: Delete key or the button. Right-click also deletes a point.
  • Undo / redo: Ctrl/⌘+Z and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z.
  • Esc clears the selection; pressed again it leaves editing.
  • Pan and zoom as usual. Points show once fewer than 1500 are in view, so zoom in on the part you are fixing.
  • Automatic clean-up settings lock once you have manual edits; Discard unlocks them.
